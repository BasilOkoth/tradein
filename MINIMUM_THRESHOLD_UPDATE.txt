V1 threshold is a MINIMUM, not an exact-match or upward-crossing trigger.
Example: threshold 9 qualifies at scores 9.00, 9.5, 10.00 or 12.00.
Initial score already above 9 qualifies without a dip below 9.
Each fresh two-purchase batch must qualify at >= configured threshold.
A second purchase in a started batch need not requalify; all settlement,
open contract, account mode, stop-on-WIN and max-trade safeguards remain.

After a fully purchased rapid pair, the next batch can trigger from a
current qualifying score without WAIT_LOW state. Open/unresolved contracts
prevent starting another pair until reconciled.
